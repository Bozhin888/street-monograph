1838 · Daguerreotype

The first person ever photographed was standing in the street

Daguerre's view of the Boulevard du Temple took minutes to expose. The traffic vanished. One man held still long enough to register: he was having his boots polished.

1888 · Roll film and the hand camera

You press the button

The first Kodak made round pictures and needed no tripod. Flash powder took Jacob Riis into the tenements at night. In 1893 Alfred Stieglitz stood for hours in a blizzard with a hand camera, waiting for one coach.
